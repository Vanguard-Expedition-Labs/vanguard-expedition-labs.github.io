Last Crew: Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Last Crew is a game for Android made by Vanguard Expedition Labs ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we don't collect personal information ourselves and there are no accounts. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads. With your consent where the law asks for it, it sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your progress, Coins, items, settings and scores are saved in a file on your phone only. We don't receive it.
  • For each purchase, the game keeps the purchase token Google Play gives it, so the same purchase is never granted twice. It stays on your phone.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all of this. The game's settings can also reset your progress.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads (watch an ad for a reward). They are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your approximate location (from your IP address),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), and how you interact with ads,
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs from the ads software.

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the game's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Ads are optional: the game can be played without ever watching one.

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which missions are too hard, where players stop and whether changes help, the game sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example a mission started or finished (its name, result, time, difficulty and how it went, such as shots fired, damage taken and items used), an ad watched, Coins earned or spent, a setting changed, or a script error. They come with an app instance ID, a Firebase installation ID, your device model, operating system, app version, country and language. They contain no name, email or contact information.

  • Where the law requires consent (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and similar places), statistics are only sent if you allowed storing information on your device and measuring content performance in the consent form. You can change this from the privacy options in the game's settings.
  • The game also asks Firebase Remote Config for settings we can change without an update (for example a reward amount), using the Firebase installation ID. Your device may be placed in test group A or B to compare two settings.
  • Google processes this data as described in Privacy and Security in Firebase. User-level data is kept for the project's Analytics retention period (2 months by default, at most 14).

Purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs and the Starter Pack are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. We may see the order number and the product bought in the Google Play Console, as Google shows them to every app developer. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children

Last Crew is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ad requests from the game are marked as not directed at children, and ads are limited to teen-rated content. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, chat or sharing between players.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites by us; we don't sell any data.
  • The game asks for no permissions beyond internet access (for ads and purchases), the advertising ID and vibration.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that matters (for example, adding analytics) will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
